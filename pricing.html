Working terms / In plain language

Know the process before we begin.

Timeline

2–10 weeks

Foundation: 2–3 weeks. Signature: 4–6 weeks. Worldbuild: 7–10 weeks, starting once content and deposit are received.

Revision rounds

2 / 3 / 4

Foundation includes 2 rounds, Signature 3, and Worldbuild 4. Additional rounds are quoted before work continues.

Code ownership

Yours at handover

After final payment, you own the approved website code and supplied brand assets. Third-party tools remain under their own licences.

Domain & hosting

Always itemised

Domain and paid third-party services are billed separately at cost. Managed hosting is included while a Care plan is active.

Milestone payments

40 / 40 / 20

40% books the project, 40% is due at design approval, and 20% is due before launch and final handover.

Post-launch support

14 / 30 / 60 days

Foundation includes 14 days of defect support, Signature 30, and Worldbuild 60. Ongoing edits and priority support are handled through Care.

Good to know

All prices are starting prices in INR. Final scope, promotion eligibility, external costs, timeline, and milestones are confirmed in writing before work begins. Displayed client-side prices are for enquiry context, not payment calculation.
