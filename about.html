Founder and creative director

Sammit Basu

Sammit founded weblo to create websites that feel considered, interactive, and unmistakably their own.

He combines creative direction, interaction design, 3D, and front-end engineering in one connected process. This keeps the original idea intact from the first conversation through design, development, and launch.

Clients work directly with the people designing and developing their project. Sammit leads the creative direction while the development team turns each idea into a dependable, high-performance experience.
